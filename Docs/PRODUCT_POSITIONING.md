# MaskID — product positioning

## Promise

MaskID is the on-device identity-protection workspace for people who need to share documents without exposing unnecessary personal data—and need a reviewable, verifiable output rather than a cosmetic black rectangle. The current workspace is for individual use; team-oriented review tools are future work.

## Access and data recovery

- New users can activate 15 calendar days of full Premium access without a
  StoreKit transaction, subscription, or automatic charge.
- Every user receives one 15-calendar-day period of full access after accepting
  the notice. The device Keychain stores its original start so reinstalling does
  not begin a new period.
- After those 15 days, a mandatory paywall offers MaskID Lifetime as the only
  purchase. Users can restore purchases, access legal/support information,
  and retrieve, export, or delete their own data. Offer-code redemption is not
  available for Lifetime and is not offered in the app.
- Existing paid entitlements remain governed by their verified expiry. New
  subscriptions and renewals are being discontinued; existing purchases retain
  the Apple terms attached to their transactions.
- Secure verified export of previously saved work remains available during
  recovery and after expiry. Expiration never deletes a local document.

## Pro

- Unlimited document processing while Pro is active.
- Batch processing and professional purpose modes.
- Advanced mask styles, image controls and custom watermarks.
- Productivity automations, Files-provider workflows, optional direct Google Drive/Dropbox import and opt-in complete iCloud backup for non-Vault documents.
- Future team-oriented review tools.

The local encrypted Vault remains part of the product; its existing biometric/PIN
authentication is preserved. Premium is positioned around repeated work: scale,
batch speed, advanced controls and moving the workflow between devices or providers.

Security correctness, privacy shielding, encryption, output verification and accessibility are product guarantees and never subscription gates.

## Language rules

- Say “verified output” only after the verifier passes.
- Say “on-device” for OCR/redaction; do not imply absolute detection accuracy.
- Never say “100% automatic”, “irreversible” without verifier context, or “anonymous”.
- Always remind users that they must review selected regions before sharing.
- Purpose presets ask what the recipient needs; they are suggestions, not silent
  redaction. Available intents include rental, employment, marketplace, travel,
  school, insurance, banking and custom.
