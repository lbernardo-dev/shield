# MaskID App Store Creative Assets

## Direction

Calm, cinematic precision: the document stays readable while the sensitive details become the user's choice. The visuals should feel like MaskID's real interface, with restrained motion and no alarmist security imagery.

## Palette

- Ink background: `#050D18`
- Deep surface: `#071426`
- Raised surface: `#0E2038`
- MaskID cyan: `#20C7D9`
- Primary type: `#F5F5F7`
- Green appears only inside genuine app UI, where it indicates a successful state.

## Typography

- Georgia Bold for the human, editorial headline.
- Avenir Next for supporting copy.
- Menlo for short process labels and status text.
- Preserve the app's original system typography inside every captured screen.

## Motion

- Silent autoplay must communicate the story without audio.
- Use gentle screen-to-screen dissolves and small camera drift.
- Keep the interface and redaction marks clear; avoid flashes, shake, or rapid zooms.
- End on the verified export state so the loop resolves on the app's main promise.

## Guardrails

- Show only real MaskID screens captured with synthetic sample documents.
- OCR is presented as a suggestion to review, not as guaranteed automatic detection.
- Do not promise anonymity, perfect detection, or irreversible redaction.
- Do not add features, customer proof, or security guarantees that are not in the app.
